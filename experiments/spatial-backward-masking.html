Spatial Backward Masking

On each trial, stare at the central fixation dot. A dim blue target square may briefly flash in the periphery, followed immediately by a ring of bright white masking squares.

Your task: Tap exactly where the target appeared. If no target was presented, tap the central fixation dot.

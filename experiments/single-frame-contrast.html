How it works

  • Keep your eyes centered on the red fixation dot.
  • At random intervals (Poisson distribution, ~5s average), a natural object image is flashed on the screen for **exactly one frame** (~16.7 ms).
  • The image's contrast level (opacity) is dynamically set using the **Quest+ adaptive procedure** to estimate your threshold.
  • If you see the image (even a faint outline or shape), immediately press the **Spacebar** or tap the **"I Saw It" button** / screen.
  • You must respond within **1.0 second**.
  • There is no feedback and the trial structure is completely hidden. Keep focusing on the red dot and report whenever you see a flash.

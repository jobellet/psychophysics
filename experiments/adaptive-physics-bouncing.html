Adaptive Physics Bouncing Balls

Welcome! This is an adaptive psychophysics tracking experiment. You will see several objects bouncing in an environment with collision sounds.

Opaque Occlusions & Odd Events

Opaque slate grey boxes will occlude parts of the canvas. The balls continue to move behind them.

At random moments, objects will experience sudden **odd events** (shifts in color, size, or speed).

Your Task

Your goal is to **touch or click on the changed object** as fast as possible within 1 second of the shift.

Successful taps score **+100 points**. False alarms or late taps deduct **-50 points**.
